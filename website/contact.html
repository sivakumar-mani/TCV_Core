A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before you say hello.

How do I choose a cable TV package?

Browse the Packages page, open the channel lists and note the pack you like. Contact us to confirm the final customer price and availability.

Is internet available at my address?

Send us your locality and PIN code. We’ll confirm coverage and the available connection options. No internet speeds or prices are published here until they are confirmed.

Can I enquire about CCTV for a shop or office?

Yes. Tell us about the space, the areas you want covered and any existing equipment, so you can discuss the next steps with our team.

What should I share for a solar enquiry?

Your property location, approximate electricity usage and available roof space are useful starting points. Our team can discuss the assessment and quotation.

Enquiry privacy
