# Time Cable Vision identity

Modern vector refinement of the supplied TCV initials, in the website's colours.

- Navy: `#111E2E`
- Lime: `#D5F36C`
- Off-white: `#FBFAF7`
- Olive detail: `#8FAD2E`

Use `tcv-logo.svg` on light backgrounds and `tcv-logo-light.svg` on dark navy backgrounds. `tcv-symbol.svg` is the compact TCV mark for profile images and small placements. `favicon.svg` uses a slightly thicker version for browser tabs.

SVG wordmarks use outlined lettering and have no font dependencies. Transparent PNG wordmarks are 1400 × 400; the transparent PNG symbol is 384 × 384. Keep proportions unchanged and leave clear space around the mark. Do not add shadows or stretch the letters.

The design keeps the TCV initials and business name while replacing the original calligraphy with clearer geometric lettering. `original-tcvlogo.png` preserves the supplied source unchanged. This is a refreshed design, not a pixel-for-pixel restoration.

Open `preview.html` in the website's `branding/` directory to compare versions.
